About the Fund

The Morgan Collier Smith Debt Fund is a private credit mortgage fund structured as an LLC. Investors own a proportional interest in the fund and participate in returns generated through a diversified portfolio of real estate-backed mortgage loans and select real estate holdings.

Why Morgan-Collier
  • Real estate-backed lending strategy
  • Quarterly income distributions
  • Professional underwriting & servicing
  • No property management responsibilities
  • Compatible with self-directed IRAs

Current Market Outlook

The lending market remains slower than historical norms. Management believes tightening liquidity may create attractive private credit and distressed lending opportunities. The fund is building capital reserves to capitalize on these opportunities while continuing to focus on income generation and principal protection.

Investor FAQ

Should I still diversify?

Absolutely. As an alternative investment, the fund is intended to complement, not replace, a diversified portfolio of equities and fixed-income securities.

Minimum investment?

$25,000 minimum investment.

How are returns paid?

Quarterly distributions can be taken as cash or reinvested for compounding growth.

Is the fund liquid?

Redemption requests are typically fulfilled within 24–48 hours, subject to liquidity and redemption amount.

How are fees structured?

1.75% annual management fee plus up to 10% participation in profits.

What does the fund invest in?

Primarily in short-term, real estate-backed bridge loans ranging from 6 months to 3 years, creating income from interest and fees.
