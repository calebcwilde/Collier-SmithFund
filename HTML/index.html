About the Fund

Credit founded on fundamentals.

A purpose-built vehicle for participation in a diversified portfolio of real estate-backed mortgage loans and select real estate holdings.

The Morgan Collier Smith Debt Fund is a private credit mortgage fund structured as an LLC. Investors own proportional interests in the fund and participate in returns generated by the underlying portfolio.

Our lending focus is straightforward: short-term bridge financing backed by real estate, with returns derived principally from interest and lending fees. The strategy is intended to complement a broadly diversified investment portfolio.

Headquartered in Lake Oswego, Oregon, with a focus on professionally underwritten and serviced mortgage investments.

Investor Information

Frequently asked questions.

An overview of the fund's investment structure, distributions, and stated terms. Current governing documents control in the event of any differences.

Should I still diversify?

Absolutely. As an alternative investment, the fund is intended to complement, not replace, a diversified portfolio of equities and fixed-income securities.

What is the minimum investment?

The stated minimum investment is $25,000, subject to current offering documents and investor eligibility requirements.

How are returns paid?

Quarterly distributions may be taken as cash or reinvested for potential compounding growth, subject to fund performance and distribution policies.

Is the fund liquid?

The investor fact sheet describes redemption requests as typically fulfilled within 24–48 hours, subject to liquidity and redemption amount. Redemptions are not guaranteed and remain subject to the fund's governing documents.

How are fees structured?

The fund's stated fee structure includes a 1.75% annual management fee plus up to 10% participation in profits. Refer to the current offering documents for full terms.

What does the fund invest in?

Primarily short-term, real estate-backed bridge loans with terms ranging from six months to three years, generating income from interest and fees.

Can I invest through a self-directed IRA?

The fund's investor materials indicate compatibility with self-directed IRAs. Eligibility and permissible investments depend on custodian requirements and applicable retirement account rules.
